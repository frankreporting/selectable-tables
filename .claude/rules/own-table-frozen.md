# The plugin's own table: frozen rows and columns

Moved here from `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-11-26. This file loads by itself when a session opens a file under `paths`. When a change alters what it says, update it here.

**Frozen rows and columns (STA-91), on a computer.** `pins.ts`, with its sums in `pin-sums.ts`. On a computer no box scrolls the table sideways (item 12), so rows stick to the note with plain `position: sticky`, where the scroll runs, and nothing runs as the note moves. The mark is read from the header line on each refresh, as for Obsidian's table.

- **One block:** the real `thead` sticks, and each frozen row under it, at `--selectable-tables-own-pin` (the scroller's top padding, negated, so rows pin at the pane's top, moved up to the start of the device pixel the pane's top falls in: where a pane starts part way into one, as under Minimal and the default theme at 78.09 px, that row of pixels showed the rows passing under; then a grip's height lower, the band below) plus the rows above. Sticky rows in one table can't leave together: each is held by its own section's end, so in the table's last few pixels a frozen body row slides under the header before both leave.
- **More blocks:** each block draws a copy of the frozen rows, rendered from their text (`render`), on a rail the block clips (`overflow-y: clip`, `overflow-x` left visible so the table's slide still clips at the box). The copy has no height and sticks; the rails' ends (`railOf`) make one block's copy slide out as the next one's slides in. The last block's rail ends the rows' height above the table's end, so they leave with it. The copy follows the table's slide by the same animation. It also carries a transform animation that moves nothing (`selectable-tables-still`, on the note's scroll): Chromium paints only what of a clipped sticky box was inside the clip when it last painted, and doesn't paint again as it sticks, so the next block's copy slid into view across a seam with its frozen column's cells drawn but their text missing, for a pinned rows' height of scrolling (Brian's hand check, 11/22). Only the screen's own frames show this (`Page.startScreencast`); `capturePage` and the page's state are right.
- **The band (STA-116):** rows pin a grip's height below the pane's top (`--table-drag-handle-size` in device pixels, 16 px, `--selectable-tables-band` on the editor), so the pinned header's column grips, which sit just above it, stay in the pane. The pinned header draws the band as its `::before`, under its grips: the real one, each block's copy and the strip's and the corner's, so it moves sideways with what draws it. It's painted with what shows behind the table, and a press on it selects the table. On the first block it grows from the header's top by `scaleY` as the table's top passes where rows pin, on the rows' timeline, so it covers just the rows passing up there: at rest it would cover the selected table's frame. A later block's lies above the block, which clips it, until the block's top passes where rows pin. A transform, where the scroll runs: the same fade by opacity drew a faint line where two blocks met inside the band. Every sum that keeps a cell clear of what's pinned counts the band: `room.height` includes it, `pinnedCell` and `readCarry` look for the pinned rows below it, and a key up into the pinned rows takes the table's top back to it (`showDown`), so the header's grips show. Phones and tablets pin nothing in the plugin's table, so they get no band, and Obsidian's table, the fallback, pins at the pane's top as before.
- **Frozen columns:** only while the table slides, a strip per block, outside the table's slide: a copy of each drawn row's frozen cells and of each spacer, in the block's order (`mirror`), each row set to its real row's measured height, so rows line up and stripe alike. A hidden cell ends each copied row, so a theme's rules for a row's last cell don't land on a frozen one. Each copy's table, the strip's, the corner's and the frozen rows' (below), sits in a `.table-wrapper` of its own, as the real table does, so a theme's rules that tell a note's table from other tables by the box holding it land on the copies too: Baseline's Row lines and Column lines draw lines on tables outside one, which doubled the copies' lines and moved each row under a short one a pixel down, and Typewriter pads cells only inside one (BMT-136). Where frozen rows and columns meet, a corner sits over the copy. The strip's first column covers the box's padding beside it with `--selectable-tables-strip`.
- **Colors:** `readPaint`, as for Obsidian's table, read before anything pins (passing over spacer rows, the header from the first block or a copy), and again after `css-change`. What pins is hidden until then. The selected table's tint lies over each piece; the frame's start edge comes from the strip and the corner. Once rows pin, the band has to stay plain, so each header that draws it goes above the table's tint and frame and takes the tint as a piece of its own (`::after`, the strip's and the corner's with the frame's start edge), and the rails of a table of more blocks go above the table's tint too, each copy having its own: before, the table's tint lay over a narrow long table's copy as well as the copy's own, and its pinned rows showed darker. At rest they stay under the tint, so its frame draws whole round the header. These switch on the rows' timeline, by main-thread animations of `z-index` and `visibility`, only while the table is selected whole.
- **Shadows (STA-41):** while cells pass under them, a soft shadow lies under the pinned rows and beside the frozen columns, 8 px deep in `--background-modifier-box-shadow`, with a line in the table's border color over the pinned cells' last pixel: where a theme draws its line there, this one lies on it, and Baseline's, drawn on the cells passing under, comes back. Each fades by an opacity animation on a timeline the scroll drives, so nothing runs as the note or the table moves. The rows' fades by a view timeline of each block's own table (`--selectable-tables-rows`, scoped to the block): in over the 16 px after the table's top passes where rows pin (the timeline's top edge is inset by the band), on the first block, and out over the 16 px before the rows leave with the table's end, on the last, by the rows' height, `--selectable-tables-pinned` on the block. The columns' is the strip's `::after`, fading in over the bar's first 24 px, and the corner's too in a table of more blocks, since the copy of the rows lies over the strip beside them. The rows' shadow rides each block's rail in its copy, and moves sideways with the table inside a box that stays put and clips it at the table's start while columns pin, since the box's padding beside the strip is outside the table. A table of one block has a rail for the shadow alone (`shadeRail`), above its real pinned rows. Every block's rail but the first starts the shadow's depth higher too (`railOf`): started only the rows' height above, the next block's shadow rode in at its top while a join crossed it, a band of its own. On a dark theme the shadow barely shows and the line marks the edge; STA-110 holds a shadow made for dark themes.
- Phones, tablets, and devices without scroll-driven animations pin nothing in the plugin's table (`usesBars`, `canFreeze`).
- **How the check script notices the shadows:** *Own 100* to *Own 109*: at rest each frozen table's picture is the same with its shadows taken out; the fades at set distances, from computed styles, which follow the timelines; a press under each shadow; an open cell in a pinned row of *Sheds* moved sideways and deep in the 2,000-row table; the joins, by where each block's shadow lies and in the screen's own frames, scrolled with real input; nothing placed again as the note scrolls; none with the switch off. `window.__checkShadesOnly`, with `__checkOwnOnly` and `__checkCellsOnly`, runs only them.

**Cells of a frozen table (STA-92).** Only real cells act; a copy shows what its real cell shows.

- **A cell stays clear of what's pinned.** `showCell` in `model.ts` measures a pinned cell where it's drawn on top (`pinnedCell` in `pins.ts`): its copy in the strip, the frozen rows' copy in the block crossing where rows pin, or the real row, which sticks itself. Any other cell is shown below the pinned rows and beside the strip (`showDown` in `pin-sums.ts`, `stripClear`), and a pinned one moves the note only once it's out of the pane, since a scroll margin there would move it on every key. With a table of more blocks wholly above the pane, the rows are drawn at its end by the last block's copy, as Obsidian's table draws them there, so a key to a pinned cell from far past the table brings the note back to show it there (STA-106). A key that went up into a frozen row, or left into a frozen column, takes the table back to its top, at where rows pin so the header's grips show, or its start (`toward`, from `decide.ts`). An open cell's caret is kept clear the same way (`showCaret`), since the cell's editor scrolls to it as if nothing were pinned.
- **A press on a copy** opens the real cell (`ROW_ATTRIBUTE`, `realRow`). Deep in a table of more blocks, a pinned row's real row is at the table's top, so `whenDrawn` puts the note's selection in the cell's text, with `writesCell` and no scroll, which has CodeMirror draw the first block, keeps that row drawn as the open cell's row is kept (`heldRow`), and opens the cell once the row is carried down to where it pins (`willOpen` and `afterPlacing` in `pins.ts`), so the cell's editor, scrolling to its caret, finds it in view. A row of the table's body still opens where it is, scrolled to. The strip's copies of the rows passing under the pinned rows show only where a pinned cell's rounded corner leaves a gap, as under Baseline, and a press there goes to the pinned row's own cell beneath, one sliding under its frozen cells, as in Obsidian's table, where the pinned rows lie above everything passing under them (`pressBeneath` in `pins.ts`).
- **The selected cell's outline and Find's marks** go on every copy too (`markCell` and `paintMatches` in `pins.ts`), and back on whenever copies are drawn anew. A match in a frozen column leaves the table where it is sideways.
- **A frozen column's open cell**, while the table slides: its row's frozen cells take the opposite of the slide, on the same timeline (`selectable-tables-lifted`), above the cells passing under them and painted solid, and the row's copy in the strip hides. The editor stays in the real cell, which the keys, the note's selection and the history follow. Only the strip's cells take presses, so a press where the copy hides reaches the open cell. Its copy's height follows the row's on each measure, as any copy's does.
- **An open cell in a frozen row and a frozen column (STA-105):** its copy can't hide, since the strip's rows pass behind its pinned rows and would show through. So while one is open, the first block's strip goes inside the table's `.table-wrapper`, under the real pinned rows, as Obsidian's own table layers them (`holdsCorner` and `placeStrip` in `pins.ts`): the wrapper's transform makes it a stacking context no real cell can rise out of, so the strip has to join it. There it takes the opposite of the slide, as the lifted cells do, and so do every pinned row's frozen cells, so the real cells show where the strip is, the open one among them, and the table still moves sideways. It moves in the same task as the rows are lifted, at a place `read` keeps for it in the wrapper, so no frame shows one without the other. With no such cell open the strip sits in the block, above the whole table, as before.
- **An open cell in a frozen row of a table of more blocks (STA-93):** its real row, in the first block, is carried down with the note while it's open (`carried` and `carry` in `pins.ts`): a scroll-driven `translateY` on the note's own scroll, between two scroll offsets `readCarry` works out on each measure (`carryRange` in `pin-sums.ts`): from where the row reaches its place under the frozen rows above it, to where the last block's copies leave with the table's end. So it lies where every block's copies pin, and moves where the scroll runs, and every block's copy of it hides. The first block, which CodeMirror keeps in the page since the note's selection is in the cell, draws above the later blocks and stops clipping up and down (`selectable-tables-carries`); its rail ends at its own end and clips its copy there instead. The editor never moves between blocks, so focus and input methods aren't touched. The row's natural place is read with its own move taken off (`laidTop`), so any frozen row in the page can be carried in the same task as its cell opens, as Enter, Tab and Shift+Tab between pinned cells do. The carried row's copies take its real height, so the copies under it follow a cell that grows. In a table that slides, its frozen cells take the opposite of the slide, with the first block's strip under the pinned rows (`holdsCorner`), deep down too. CodeMirror moves the note for a change of height above its scroll anchor, which keeps the rows on screen still as the cell grows; Undo's own scroll into view skips that, so an Undo that shrinks the cell moves the rows under the pinned ones by the cell's change. Each refresh passes the open cell's row as it is now to the pins (`refresh` in `model.ts`), so rows put in or taken out above it from elsewhere keep the carried row, the lifted cells and the hidden copies on it (STA-106).
- **The freeze commands and the right-click items** (`freeze-commands.ts`) take their cell from the selected cell or `openCells`, close an open cell through `escapeCell`, and write as for Obsidian's table. A right-click on a cell of the plugin's table, or on a copy, makes a menu with `Menu.forEvent` (`tableMenus`, which `tables.ts` registers; item 14 of the list in `own-table.md`); inside an open cell, the borrowed editor fires `editor-menu`. After a freeze command, Undo or Redo with a cell selected, the cell is shown once what pins is placed (`afterPlacing`), and again right after CodeMirror's own scroll for Undo and Redo, a few frames later, through `EditorView.scrollHandler`, which lets that scroll go ahead. On a phone or tablet, where nothing pins in the plugin's table, a freeze gives the notice of a device that can't pin.
- **How the check script notices:** *Own 65* to *Own 73*, *Own 82* to *Own 86* for STA-93, and *Own 87* to *Own 91* for STA-106, each case against the switch off (`window.__checkCornersOnly`, with the three above, runs only those): the keys through *Plants*, a press half under the pinned rows, presses on the pinned header and the frozen column moved sideways, typing and Tab there, Find in the strip, the commands, Undo and Redo against the switch off, both right-click menus, a stand-in for Baseline's Row lines (*Own 201*), the 2,000-row table's pinned header pressed deep down, opening in place, growing, past three seams, past the table's end, Undo and Redo, the keys between pinned cells, and its outline across seams, then 60 seeded random steps, the copies checked against the text after each. `window.__checkFrozenCellsOnly`, with `__checkOwnOnly` and `__checkCellsOnly`, runs only them. `.dev/measure`'s `measure-sta92.js` times typing in a frozen table's cells against the same table unfrozen, and a freeze command on 2,000 rows.
